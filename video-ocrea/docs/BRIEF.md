# Brief — Ocrea launch film

- **Product (fictional):** Ocrea, a phone app that turns one reference image and one sentence into a
  finished short film. Mock brand, no real logos.
- **Purpose / audience:** launch film for creators and small brands, shown on screens and in
  landscape feeds.
- **Format:** 16:9 only, 1920x1080, 60 fps, 28.0 s (1680 frames). Composition `OcreaLandscape`.
- **Language on screen:** French (user's choice, option B). The skill normally does English only; the
  user accepted the exception. Every character stays inside the allowed Latin set (é, è, à, ê, ç, '
  and the em dash are allowed; no "œ").
- **World:** dark and warm. Black to deep brown, terracotta and amber light, dust in the air, the
  camera always moving. Every hero is a lit 3D mesh with real thickness.
- **Words on screen (written by Claude):**
  - `Ocrea` (wordmark)
  - `Votre prochain film tient dans la main.` — accent *main*
  - prompt typed in the app: `Un road trip au coucher du soleil, en 35 mm.`
  - `Chaque plan trouve sa place.` — accent *place*
  - `Prêt à ouvrir.` — accent *ouvrir*
  - `Tout le cinéma, sans le plateau.` — accent *cinéma*
  - CTA slab: `Créez votre premier film — gratuit`
- **Call to action:** Créez votre premier film — gratuit.
- **Brand colour / logo:** none given. Palette chosen: black #070504, deep brown #1a0f0a,
  terracotta #c2562f, amber accent #ffb347. Logo: an abstract amber swoosh drawn in code.
- **References:** none given; the user's prompt is the brief.
- **Audio:** none given. The film is silent; a second-by-second list of where sounds would go is
  delivered with the film. No voiceover.
